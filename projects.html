AI Customer Support Chatbot (RAG)

A chatbot that answers customer questions on shipping, returns, payments, and products from a store's documents, citing each source.

Problem. Support staff repeat answers, and general chatbots invent policies.

How it works. The question is rewritten using chat history, matched against a ChromaDB index of local embeddings, and answered by Llama 3 on Groq from the retrieved passages.

Real-world use. First-line shop support. The demo uses a fictional store, ShopSmart BD.

VisualMind AI

An app that describes an uploaded image and answers follow-up questions about it.

Problem. A caption alone leaves questions open, and text in images is easy to miss.

How it works. BLIP writes a caption, then Llama 4 Scout on Groq analyses the image and answers follow-up questions. It accepts JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF files up to 4 MB.

Real-world use. Alt text, reading screenshots, and checking photos.

HireTrack AI

A job tracker that scores a resume against a job description, drafts tailored documents, and tracks each application to an offer.

Problem. Applicants rarely know why screening rejects a resume.

How it works. A local scoring engine rates a resume on skills, experience, education, and keywords with no API call, and lists missing skills. A language model drafts a tailored resume and cover letter, and Exa AI finds listings.

Real-world use. Checking a resume before applying and seeing recurring skill gaps.

No. 04 · GitHub

Anomaly Detection in IoMT Devices

A pipeline that detects and classifies attacks in network traffic from connected medical devices.

Problem. Medical devices are attack targets, and attacks are rare in the data.

How it works. A variational autoencoder (trained on about 171,000 normal records) and an Isolation Forest score how unusual each record is. A TabNet classifier uses the scores to assign the attack type, on about 2.68 million training and 892,000 test records.

Real-world use. A hospital network monitor.

Status. Research code, not deployed.

No. 06 · GitHub

Chittagong Weather Prediction

A classifier that predicts Chittagong weather as rain, high temperature, normal temperature, or cold, with a web interface.

Problem. Weather matters for farming, transport, and disaster management.

How it works. Trains on daily temperature, rainfall, humidity, and sunshine records from a public Mendeley dataset, using SMOTE for class balance. A stacking ensemble of Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, SVM, and Logistic Regression is tuned by randomized search and cross-validation. A Flask app returns the class with a confidence score.

Real-world use. Planning around rain or heat in farming or transport.

Status. A student project, not an operational forecast.
