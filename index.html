Current Research

Generative-Enhanced Ensemble Framework for Diabetic Retinopathy Grading under Severe Class Imbalance

Manuscript in Preparation
ELITE Research Lab LLC, New York
August 2025 - Present

Developing a dual-head architecture combining ordinal regression with categorical classification for seven-stage diabetic retinopathy grading. The framework utilizes Stable Diffusion 2.1 generative augmentation with ControlNet-Canny conditioning and morphological attention masks.

Key Achievements:

  • Achieved 91.65% accuracy for seven-stage DR classification
  • Implemented stacking ensemble combining five CNN architectures
  • Improved baseline accuracy by 2.09% with 91.67% macro F1-score
  • Validated through Grad-CAM explainability analysis
PyTorch Stable Diffusion 2.1 ControlNet CNN Ensemble Medical Imaging
