I'm Auntor Chakma, a Researcher and Research Assistant at ELITE Research Lab. I work on trustworthy multimodal AI. My recent papers cover federated learning and explainable AI for healthcare, and I am moving toward vision-language models and AI safety. Within that work I study four properties of the models, interpretability, trustworthiness, AI safety, and privacy for data security.

I grew up in Rangamati, in the Chittagong Hill Tracts, where specialist medical care is hard to reach. The lung-sound project I supervise aims at a tool that a health worker in a low-resource clinic could run and understand.

My recent papers cover diabetic retinopathy classification from fundus images, Bangla license plate recognition, and a review of multimodal federated learning in healthcare. My B.Sc. thesis was on intrusion detection for Internet of Medical Things networks. Project code is on GitHub, and three live demos are linked under Projects.

I am a Researcher and Research Assistant at ELITE Research Lab (New York, remote), and Management Information System (MIS) Officer on the World Food Programme (WFP)-funded Mother and Child Benefit Programme and Natural Disaster Risk Reduction Project, run by ASHIKA Development Associates in Rangamati. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Rangamati Science and Technology University (2025).

Currently seeking: research-based graduate positions in Computer Science, with a focus on multimodal AI, vision-language models, trustworthy AI, and AI safety, open to both master's and doctoral programmes. Supervisors and collaborators can reach me by email.

Mentors

My current mentor and principal investigator is Md Kishor Morol, a PhD student at Cornell University and a Researcher at Meta FAIR, who also founded ELITE Research Lab. He supervised my work on multimodal and federated learning. He now advises me on vision-language models, their applications, and AI safety, and on formal privacy guarantees.
